EO is a browser-based situational-awareness platform that combines satellite imagery, weather, and public reports on one live map.

- Let users geotag incidents with photos, descriptions, and severity levels.
- Overlay disaster, traffic, weather, and environmental data by region and time.
- Provide role-based dashboards for residents, responders, and municipal coordinators.
- Generate accessible alerts and shareable situation reports with verified sources.
- Protect sensitive location data through redaction rules and granular permissions.
